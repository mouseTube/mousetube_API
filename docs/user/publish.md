# Publish with a DOI

**Publishing** deposits the files of a shared session in a repository (Zenodo) and gives them a **DOI**: a
permanent identifier that you can cite and that anyone can resolve to your data.

## Before you start

- The session is **Shared** (public). A session waiting for validation cannot be published yet.
- It does not use an entry that an administrator has not validated yet. The Visibility card says so.
- It has at least one file ready.
- A **protocol** is chosen.

## Publish

On the **File** step, or in *My vocalizations*, choose **Publish in a repository**. A window shows a **preview
of the description** that will appear on Zenodo: title, description, keywords, people and license. Check it,
edit what you want, and confirm.

mouseTube then sends the files in the background. You are told when it is done, and the session becomes
<span class="pill red">Published</span> with its DOI.

!!! info "You do not need a Zenodo account"
    mouseTube makes the deposit for you, in its own space on Zenodo, with the description you checked, including the
    people named on the record (you can edit them in the preview).

## Publish when it is validated

If your session has to wait for an administrator, you can ask for it to be **published automatically** once it
is validated.

1. In the *Visibility* card of a draft, tick **Publish automatically once validated**, then share the session.
2. Because it has to wait, a **preview of the publication** opens. Read it, edit it if you want, tick
   *I checked the preview and want it published automatically once validated* and choose
   **Approve and publish after validation**.
3. When an administrator validates the session, it is published **as you approved it**, and you receive an
   e-mail with the DOI.

If what would be published changed in the meantime (an administrator corrected an entry), nothing goes out:
you are told, and you publish it yourself with a new preview. You can **Cancel automatic publication** at any
time while it waits.

## A published session keeps living

You can still **edit** a published session: the description and the keywords of its record follow, with the same
DOI. **Files you add afterwards** make a **new version** of the record, with its own DOI; the whole keeps one
global DOI that always points to the latest version. A file of a published version cannot be removed.

## Cite your data

Cite the **DOI** of the version you used, or the global DOI for the whole. The DOI is shown on the card of each
file in **Vocalizations**.
